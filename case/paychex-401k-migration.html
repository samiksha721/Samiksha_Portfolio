Case 02 · Paychex IT Solutions · Shipped

Migrating 360 file-based recordkeeping integrations to API-based

I led the migration of 360 file-based 401(k) recordkeeping integrations to an API-based platform serving 100K+ users. The real product work was sequencing that migration — and it cut vendor onboarding turnaround by 70%.

RoleProduct Owner, ePlan Services
Scale100K+ users · 360 file-based integrations
DomainHR/payroll · 401(k) retirement plans
TimelineJul 2023 – Nov 2024

Product strategy breakdown

01

Context

ePlan Services is Paychex's 401(k) retirement platform. Recordkeeping — the system of record for participants' retirement accounts — ran on a legacy setup, connected to a large network of vendors through 360 file-based integrations.

02

Problem

Onboarding a vendor onto the legacy recordkeeping system was slow. With hundreds of integrations to move, a migration done in the wrong order would stall on dependencies — and in retirement plans, errors aren't an option.

03

Who it's for

Plan participants

100K+ people whose retirement records must stay accurate through the move.

Integration vendors

Need a faster, clearer path onto the platform.

04

Strategic bet

In a migration, the sequence is the strategy. Get the integration order right and speed follows; get it wrong and everything waits on everything else.

Moving to an API-based platform made vendor onboarding faster by design. Sequencing the move of all 360 file-based integrations to API determined how quickly that benefit arrived.

05

How I prioritised

Prioritisation lens
  • Customer impact
  • Business value
  • Technical dependencies
Constraint
  • Compliance with retirement-plan standards on every delivery — non-negotiable, not a trade-off
06

How it worked

TranslateBusiness and engineering needs into Epics, PRDs and BRDs
Define doneClear acceptance criteria on every requirement
Sequence360 file-based integrations moved to API, ordered by impact, value and dependency
AlignOne backlog across engineering, UX and client-facing teams
07

Results

−70%Vendor onboarding turnaround
360File-based integrations migrated to API
100K+Users served by the new API-based platform
08

Risks & trade-offs

Data integrity. Recordkeeping is people's retirement money; a migration error is a trust and compliance failure, not a bug.

Dependency chains. With 360 integrations, one blocked vendor can hold up many others.

Vendors move at different speeds. Each file-based vendor has to be ready to switch to API before its integration can move.

09

Learning

In regulated platforms, precise requirements are the product. Clear acceptance criteria are what let a big migration move fast and stay compliant.
