Signal Desk — an AI campaign builder that shows its maths

A marketer describes a goal; Signal Desk proposes a campaign built from location, transaction and behavioural signals — and puts a transparent unit-economics model next to it, showing cost against value for every campaign, so nobody mistakes a confident number for a true one.

Product strategy breakdown

01

Context

This picks up where my Zeta Global work left off. Marketing platforms sit on rich first-party signals, and AI campaign builders are now table stakes. Most of them output an audience and a projected return with no way to see how it was reached.

02

Problem

Black-box projections create false precision. A campaign can show strong ROAS while adding little incremental margin — it reached people who would have bought anyway.

03

Who it's for

Campaign managers

Need a strong first draft fast, without a data-science queue.

Marketing leads & finance

Need to defend the spend — on margin, not just on ROAS.

04

Strategic bet

Transparency is the differentiator. A campaign builder that exposes its economics wins the trust of the people who sign off on budget.

05

Scope decisions

In
  • Campaign proposals from three signal types
  • A visible economic model behind every output
  • A written drawbacks and guardrails analysis
  • Next: LLM-generated subject lines and content
Out
  • A single headline ROAS with no model behind it
  • Real customer data — simulated datasets only
  • Autonomous launch — a person approves every campaign
06

How it works

BriefMarketer sets the goal in the web app
Buildn8n webhook runs the AI logic over the signals
ExplainAudience, channel and the economics behind them
ApproveA person signs off before anything runs
07

Metrics I'd hold it to

North starIncremental margin per campaign

Not ROAS — ROAS rewards reaching people who'd buy anyway.

AdoptionProposals launched with light edits · time to first draft
GuardrailsConsent coverage · disparate-impact checks · cost per campaign
08

Risks & trade-offs

False precision. A clean number invites more trust than the data supports.

Privacy. Location and transaction data fall under GDPR, CCPA and India's DPDP Act — consent and purpose limits shape what's allowed.

Disparate impact. Targeting on behavioural signals can exclude groups unfairly without anyone intending it.

Operational controls. Approval, spend caps and audit trails before any of this runs unattended.

09

Outcome & learning

Writing the drawbacks section first made the product better — every guardrail became a feature, not a footnote.
