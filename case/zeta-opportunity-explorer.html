Case 03 · Zeta Global · Shipped

Opportunity Explorer: turning raw ad data into campaigns that launch

On the Zeta Marketing Platform I took a set of marketing-intelligence tools from idea to launch for SMB clients. In the first year, clients activated 37% faster and launched 115% more campaigns.

RoleProduct Manager / Business Analyst
ScopeRoadmaps for 3 platform tools
CustomersSMB clients · B2B SaaS
TimelineJan 2019 – Dec 2022

Product strategy breakdown

01

Context

The Zeta Marketing Platform is a B2B SaaS marketing-intelligence platform. Opportunity Explorer is a set of tools within it that help clients find and act on campaign opportunities, built on large volumes of programmatic advertising data.

02

Problem

Raw programmatic data isn't a decision. SMB clients had the data but not a quick way to turn it into a campaign — which slowed client activation and kept campaign volume down.

03

Who it's for

SMB marketing clients

Small teams that need insight they can act on, not a data project.

Enterprise clients

Served by AI-assisted workflows to lift engagement and campaign effectiveness.

04

Strategic bet

Activation is the growth lever. Close the gap between data and a launched campaign, and usage follows.

05

What I built

Product
  • Roadmaps for 3 Opportunity Explorer tools, ideation through launch
  • A reporting tool that turns raw programmatic data into campaign insight
  • AI-assisted workflows with engineering, data science and marketing
Planning
  • Co-owned quarterly and annual release plans
  • Product decisions validated with experiments and engagement studies
06

How decisions got made

HypothesiseWhat would help a client launch sooner?
TestUser experiments and engagement studies
AnalyseResults read in Tableau and Looker
ShipInto the quarterly release plan
07

Results

+115%Campaign activations in the first year
37%Faster turnaround on client activation
3Platform tools taken from idea to launch

Experiments also improved customer retention and satisfaction.

08

Risks & trade-offs

Simplicity vs depth. SMB clients need answers, not every dimension of the data — every added view risks slowing activation down again.

Data quality. Insight built on raw programmatic data is only as good as that data.

09

Learning

Data products win on the step after the insight.
