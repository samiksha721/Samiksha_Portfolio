How I build product

Five habits that show up in every case study

01

Let behaviour decide

Heap data, experiments and engagement studies settle debates faster than opinions do.

02

The support queue is a roadmap

Ticket analysis told me what to make self-serve. Root-cause analysis of defects told me what to fix first.

03

Sequence is strategy

In a migration or a roadmap, the order you ship in decides the outcome as much as what you ship.

04

Show the economics

From a category P&L to a campaign's cost vs value, every product gets a business case.

05

Prototype before you argue

A working LLM-agent prototype answers "will this work?" faster than a deck — and shows honestly where AI doesn't earn its cost.
